🚀 Getting started

CourseCraft works two ways — pick whichever suits you.

On the web (nothing to install)

  1. Open coursecraft.sweeeter.com in any browser.
  2. Tap Create account, enter an email and a password (6+ characters). You're in.
  3. Already have an account? Enter your email and password and tap Sign in.

On Android (the app)

  1. On your phone, open the download link your host shared and tap the file to install.
  2. If Android warns about "unknown sources," tap Settings → Allow, then Install.
  3. Open CourseCraft and sign in (or create an account) — same as the web.
Everyone starts as a learner. You can switch on creator tools any time — see Become a creator.

🎓 For learners watch & learn

Find a course, watch the lectures, and test yourself.

Browse and enroll

  1. From the home screen, tap Browse courses.
  2. Tap any course to see its lessons.
  3. Tap Enroll to add it to your My Learning list. (Tap again to unenroll.)

Watch lectures

  1. Inside a course, tap a lecture (marked ▶) to play it.
  2. Your position is saved automatically — come back later and it resumes where you left off.
  3. On the home screen, ▶ Continue jumps straight back to your last lecture.
If a YouTube video won't play on an older phone, tap Open in new tab / Open in YouTube. Self-hosted videos play in the app on any device.

Take assessments & see leaderboards

  1. In a course, tap Assessments & leaderboards.
  2. Tap Take, answer the questions, and tap Submit — it's graded instantly.
  3. You'll see your score and whether you passed. Retake to improve — your best score counts.
  4. Tap Leaderboard to see the top scorers.

Join a live session

  1. When a class is live, a 🔴 Live now banner appears on the home screen — tap Join.
  2. Or open a course → Live sessions → Join (live) or Watch recording (after it ends).

🧑‍🏫 For creators teach

Build a course from video lessons and quizzes, then publish it and even teach live.

Become a creator

  1. On the home screen, tap Become a creator. That's it — creator tools appear.

Create a course

  1. Tap Create a course, enter a title (subject and level are optional), tap Create.
  2. You're taken to the course's Manage screen.

Add lessons

  1. Add a section (e.g. "Getting Started") — type a title and tap Add section.
  2. Under a section, add a lecture: enter a title and a video (see Video options), tap Add video lecture.
  3. Repeat to build out your course.

Add a quiz (assessment)

  1. On the Manage screen tap Assessments → enter a title and a pass mark (%) → Create assessment.
  2. Tap Add question. Type the question, pick a type (single choice, multiple, or true/false), set points.
  3. Fill in the options and tick the correct one(s). Tap Add question. Repeat.

Publish

  1. When ready, tap Publish course. Published courses appear in the catalog for learners to enroll.

Host a live session

  1. On the Manage screen tap Live sessions.
  2. Enter a session title, a video link (a YouTube Live link, or your own stream URL — see below), and a start date/time. Tap Schedule.
  3. When it's time, tap Go live — learners can now join. Tap End when finished; the recording is saved automatically as a replay.

See how you're doing

  1. Tap Analytics for enrollment counts and, per quiz, the number of attempts and the average score.

🎬 Video options

When adding a lecture or scheduling a live session, the video field accepts either of these:

  • YouTube — paste a YouTube video ID or full link (for live, a YouTube Live link). Free and easy; best on modern devices.
  • Self-hosted — paste a direct .mp4 link or an .m3u8 (HLS) stream URL from your own storage or live server. These play right inside the app on any device, including older phones where YouTube may not.
Self-hosting keeps everything in-house at no cost — e.g. a free stream server for live, or free file storage for recorded lessons. Ask your administrator for the stream/file URL to paste in.

💡 Tips & troubleshooting

  • Sent back to the sign-in screen? The app may have been idle a while — just sign in again.
  • A video won't play? Use Open in new tab / Open in YouTube, or ask the creator for a self-hosted version.
  • Moving around: every screen has ← Back (previous screen) and ⌂ Home at the top.
  • Change your name: tap Edit name on the home screen — it's what shows on leaderboards.
  • Web or app? Both share the same account and data — sign in on either.
